Plant GRO-seq sequence model and evaluation across three species.

a, Plant GRO-seq datasets and strand-resolved nascent transcription. b, Sequence model: 1,024-bp one-hot DNA inputs produce two 0-1 window scores. c, g, k, Representative held-out regions of Arabidopsis thaliana, Triticum aestivum and Zea mays (40, 80 and 80 kb). Experimental tracks show log2(1 + mean CPM) in 128-bp bins, counting aligned SE/read-1 5-prime endpoints; CPM is calculated independently per SRX and averaged equally. Prediction tracks retain raw window scores, with a 128-bp sliding step and display in the central 128 bp. Gray bins mark unavailable DNA inputs and are not zero-filled. d, e, h, i, l, m, Plus- and minus-strand correlations over all 45,066, 98,656 and 49,786 formal test windows, respectively. Both mean CPM and original predicted scores are transformed by log2(1 + value) before Pearson r; rho denotes Spearman correlation. Dashed lines are OLS fits. All source scatter points, zero-origin axes and relative within-panel density shading are retained. f, j, n, HiLearn 0.2.2 ROC and PR curves pooling both strands (micro). Positives overlap same-strand consensus intervals supported by at least two SRX by at least 1 bp. Curves use raw scores; PR AUC is the native trapezoidal area including artificial endpoints, not average precision. Track examples were selected for experimental peaks and agreement. Evaluation uses held-out genomic sequences from the experiments that supplied training labels; it is not independent-library validation.
