Cross-species prediction and evaluation of plant GRO-seq signals.

a-c, Radar profiles for input DNA from Arabidopsis thaliana, Zea mays and Triticum aestivum. Pearson r compares log2(1 + experimental mean CPM) with log2(1 + raw model score), pooling both strands across all formal test windows. Micro AUROC pools strand-specific labels and raw scores (HiLearn 0.2.2; seed 42). Positive 1,024-bp windows overlap a same-strand consensus region by at least 1 bp; consensus requires at least two distinct SRX experiments. Micro Jaccard at the 85th percentile (J85) is sum(TP)/sum(TP + FP + FN), pooling strand counts. High-signal windows strictly exceed thresholds computed independently for each signal, target species and strand. Pooled metrics do not average strand-specific metrics; n counts strand-window pairs, not biological replicates. Axes show unrescaled values on a common 0-1 display scale, with 0.25 grid steps; polygon area is not a composite score. Coral red and deep indigo indicate same-species and cross-species models. Circles, triangles and squares identify Arabidopsis, maize and wheat source models. d-f, Selected 100-kb regions. Experimental tracks are coral red and cross-species predictions are deep indigo, irrespective of strand. Experimental tracks show log2(1 + mean CPM) in 128-bp bins after per-SRX normalization and equal averaging. Raw predictions use 1,024-bp inputs at 128-bp steps and are displayed over the central 128 bp. Regional r uses log2(1+x) for both full-window mean CPM and raw scores across common valid 1,024-bp inputs. Own-model r refers to the same-species prediction. Invalid DNA windows remain gaps. Coordinates are 0-based, end-exclusive. Regions were selected conditionally on local performance and are illustrative; a-c summarize all formal test windows.
