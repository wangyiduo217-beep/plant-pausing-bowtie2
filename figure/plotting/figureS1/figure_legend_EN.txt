Regional correlation and experimental mean signal across plant species and strands.

(a, b) Arabidopsis thaliana; (c, d) Triticum aestivum; (e, f) Zea mays. Left column: plus strand; right column: minus strand. Green/blue bars use the left axis (Pearson r); orange points and connecting lines use the right axis (mean experimental CPM). Pearson r is calculated across all 1,024-bp test windows in each region between log2(1+mean SRX CPM) and log2(1+predicted score). The orange values are arithmetic means of the original per-window mean SRX CPM, including zeros; the right-axis means are not log-transformed. Each SRX is normalized independently to CPM before equal-weight averaging (14, 5 and 3 SRX for Arabidopsis, wheat and maize). Both axes use common limits across all six panels: 0-1 for r and 0-6 CPM for mean signal. Up means upstream and Down means downstream, defined relative to the original canonical protein-coding transcript reference. Distances are in kb. Counts below category names are the full regional window counts n, shared by the two strands; no data are omitted. Total test windows are 45,066, 98,656 and 49,786, respectively. The same eight region assignments, values and models as the two-page version are retained; only the layout and category-label abbreviations have changed. The line connects category summaries as a visual guide. No score threshold, outlier removal, invented replicate/error bars or significance tests are used. These summaries describe covariation and do not establish a causal effect of signal intensity. strand_seiplant_v1; repeats retained. Ordinary text is 7-pt Arial; panel letters are 8-pt Arial Bold.
