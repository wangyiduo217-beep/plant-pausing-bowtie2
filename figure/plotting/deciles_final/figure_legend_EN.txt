a-c, Arabidopsis, wheat and maize. Within each Q1-Q10 group, green (left) and blue (right) points show the previously calculated plus- and minus-strand Pearson correlations, respectively. Whiskers are the original 95% confidence intervals from 1,000 chromosome-stratified, paired 100-kb block-bootstrap replicates. Two color-coded rows above each panel give the exact strand-specific window counts. The strands were neither pooled nor re-ranked jointly: positive-CPM windows retain their original ten equal-count rank groups within each species and strand. Thus matching Q labels indicate relative rank within each strand, not necessarily identical absolute CPM intervals. Both experiment (mean SRX CPM) and original predicted score were transformed by log2(1 + value) before calculating correlations, matching the overall-test analysis. Zero-CPM windows remain separate, with undefined correlations (NA). No statistics or input values were changed. strand_seiplant_v1; repeats retained; Arabidopsis/wheat/maize use 14/5/3 SRX, including the original five-library wheat model.
